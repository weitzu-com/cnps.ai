# The business buyer’s AI procurement checklist

Start with a business task and an acceptance test. A product category, model name or demonstration is not a complete requirement.

## Define the job

- Which task should improve, and who performs it today?
- What is the current cost, time or error rate? Keep a baseline.
- Who will use the system, approve the trial and sign off on procurement?
- What country, languages, working environment and data constraints apply?
- What would make the project unsuitable even if the demonstration works?

## Ask for evidence that matches the decision

| Evidence | Useful for | Still needs checking |
|---|---|---|
| Official product documentation | Features and supported configurations | The exact SKU, region and version |
| Open-source repository | Interfaces and deployment approach | Reproducibility, licensing and maintenance |
| Vendor customer story | A reported use case | Measurement method and applicability |
| Demonstration video | Understanding a possible workflow | Failure conditions and repeatability |
| Your own pilot | Testing the actual requirement | Representative data and agreed acceptance |

An industry reference on this site is not a claim that CNPS delivered the project or represents the vendor.

## Compare the whole quotation

Include hardware, accessories, subscriptions, model or speech services, installation, training, logistics, destination charges, support and replacement costs. Identify which party owns each responsibility. Check what changes if usage doubles or only half the expected users adopt the system.

For connected devices, ask what happens when the network fails or a service ends. For software, ask how documents, accounts and data are exported or deleted. For robots and camera systems, name the qualified owner of site integration and safe operation.

## Agree on a small, useful pilot

Record the inputs, configuration, test conditions, success metrics and failures. Include difficult examples. Agree on acceptance before running the test, and keep an explicit list of excluded work.

For China recording hardware destined for UAE or KSA, use the [China→Gulf sourcing method](/guides/china-to-gulf-ai-recording-sourcing) after the workflow and sample gates — not a bulk PO from a brochure.

## Prepare your request

Share your company, destination country, task, pilot quantity or user count, target timeline and main constraints. Availability, commercial terms and implementation scope are confirmed during quotation.
