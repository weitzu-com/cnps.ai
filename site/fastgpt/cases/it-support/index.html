How to evaluate a similar workflow

Start with one support topic and an approved FAQ. Evaluate cited answers, missing-information handling and a ticket draft reviewed by the employee.

What to measure

Correct answers, supported citations, appropriate handoffs and total handling time including review.

The data and users in scope, access controls, the review process, integration dependencies and acceptance samples. Your written proposal defines deliverables, costs and timing.
