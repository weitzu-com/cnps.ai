CNPS pilot concept

How to evaluate a similar workflow

Evaluate routine product or service questions with one approved FAQ set. Route account-specific, uncertain and high-impact requests to a person.

What to measure

Answer quality, appropriate handoff and time per correctly resolved request. Handoff reduction alone is not the goal.

What we agree before starting

The data and users in scope, access controls, the review process, integration dependencies and acceptance samples. Your written proposal defines deliverables, costs and timing.
