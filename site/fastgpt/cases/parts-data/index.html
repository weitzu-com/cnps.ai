How to evaluate a similar workflow

Use synthetic or approved RFQs to extract the original part number, requested quantity and missing information. Match only against approved product data; a person confirms substitutions and pricing.

What to measure

Field-level correctness, missing-field detection, traceability to the input and review time.

What we agree before starting

The data and users in scope, access controls, the review process, integration dependencies and acceptance samples. Your written proposal defines deliverables, costs and timing.
