Before your first conversation

A few useful answers.

Can we assess a deployment in our own environment?

Yes. We start with your data and infrastructure requirements. Model endpoints, parsing, storage, backups and integrations are all included in the scope review.

What should we bring to a pilot discussion?

One workflow, a process owner, your existing systems and a few non-confidential examples. Please do not send credentials or production customer data in your initial inquiry.

Are these CNPS customer projects?

The reference cases are published by FastGPT. CNPS provides its own pilot concepts alongside the original sources. Results have not been independently audited by CNPS.

How are pricing and timelines agreed?

In a written proposal after scope and dependencies are understood. Platform licensing, model usage and implementation services are identified separately where applicable.
